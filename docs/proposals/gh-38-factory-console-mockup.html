Factory Floor · mobile first, in Factorio's design language

Four screens. A passcode gate first, because the console is reached over Tailscale. The overview carries only what changes what you do next: alerts that need a person, machines that are assembling, and today's output. Every agent is an assembling machine with a status light, every role a module slot, every factory a surface. Detail is one tap away. The belt under every title bar carries what the factory has actually produced, the inserter at its end never stops, working machines show crafting progress, and the chimneys behind this page are the watchers.
